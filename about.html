About us

Who we are and what we do

Our mission

JobFinder was created to help young people in Kazakhstan start their careers. Many students and graduates do not know where to look for their first job, and many companies cannot find motivated employees.

Our platform brings them together in one place: job seekers can easily find vacancies, and employers can quickly find the right candidates.

Why choose JobFinder

  • All vacancies are checked by our team
  • Jobs for students without experience and internships
  • Companies from different cities and fields
  • Free for job seekers
